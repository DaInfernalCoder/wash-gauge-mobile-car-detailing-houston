# Wash Gauge design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers who want service scope matched to current condition instead of a one-size-fits-all promise. The page should explain that calibration and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Gauge ink — `#132529`
- Dial teal — `#224147`
- Rinse aqua — `#4BB4BD`
- Workshop white — `#F4F7F3`
- Needle orange — `#E66C3E`
- Display: Barlow Condensed
- Body: Manrope

## Layout thesis

The route frame becomes a condition gauge: observe the vehicle, set the useful scope, schedule, and review.

## Signature

A restrained semicircular gauge replaces the inherited temperature path and appears only at decision points.

## Critique

The obvious idea used dashboard clutter. The revision keeps one dial with four marks so condition remains understandable at a glance.
